Professional Summary

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Geomatics Engineering at the University of Isfahan, Iran. My research focuses on the application of machine learning and deep learning to geospatial data analysis, Earth observation, and remote sensing. In particular, my work involves developing intelligent methods for environmental monitoring and assessment using multisource and multisensor geospatial data.

I received my Ph.D. (Dr.-Ing.) in Data Science in Earth Observation—formerly known as Signal Processing in Earth Observation—from the Technical University of Munich (TUM), Germany. I have authored and co-authored numerous scientific publications in international journals and conference proceedings. I also teach and supervise undergraduate, master’s, and doctoral students in AI, remote sensing, geospatial data mining, photogrammetry, image analysis, and related fields.
